================================================================================
HOSTINGER EMAIL SETUP GUIDE FOR AFORMIX (no-reply@aformix.com)
================================================================================

This guide explains how to connect your Hostinger email (no-reply@aformix.com)
to the Aformix Authentication & Transactional Email System.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXACT .ENV CONFIGURATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open your "frontend/.env" file (or ".env.local") and paste the following block
at the bottom of the file:

# ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# AFORMIX APP & HOSTINGER SMTP CONFIGURATION
# ------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Application URL (Used to generate links in verification & reset emails)
APP_URL="https://www.aformix.com"

# Hostinger SMTP Settings
SMTP_HOST="smtp.hostinger.com"
SMTP_PORT=465
SMTP_USER="no-reply@aformix.com"
SMTP_PASSWORD="YOUR_HOSTINGER_EMAIL_PASSWORD_HERE"
SMTP_FROM_EMAIL="no-reply@aformix.com"
SMTP_FROM_NAME="Aformix"


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO GET YOUR HOSTINGER EMAIL CREDENTIALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If you haven't created the "no-reply@aformix.com" mailbox yet in Hostinger:

Step 1: Log in to your Hostinger hPanel (https://hpanel.hostinger.com).
Step 2: In the left sidebar or dashboard, click on "Emails".
Step 3: Select your domain: "aformix.com".
Step 4: Click "Create Email Account" (or "Add Email Account").
Step 5: Set the email username to "no-reply" (so full address is no-reply@aformix.com).
Step 6: Create a strong password and remember it.
Step 7: Copy that password and paste it into "SMTP_PASSWORD" in your .env file.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOSTINGER SMTP SERVER DETAILS REFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hostinger Standard Outgoing SMTP Settings:
- SMTP Host Server : smtp.hostinger.com
- Port             : 465 (SSL) or 587 (TLS)
- Authentication   : Required (Yes)
- Username         : no-reply@aformix.com  (must be the full email address)
- Password         : Your Hostinger mailbox password


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW TO TEST AND VERIFY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Save your ".env" file.
2. Restart your development server:
     npm run dev
3. Open your browser and navigate to:
     http://localhost:3000/register
4. Register a test account with your personal email address.
5. Check your inbox: you will receive the 6-digit Aformix verification code
   sent directly from no-reply@aformix.com!
6. Complete email verification: you will receive the official Aformix Welcome Email.
7. Test "Forgot Password" on http://localhost:3000/forgot-password:
   you will receive the password reset link from no-reply@aformix.com.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TROUBLESHOOTING TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Error: "Invalid login: 535 5.7.0 Authentication failed"
  -> Check that your SMTP_PASSWORD matches your Hostinger webmail password.
  -> Make sure SMTP_USER is the full email address: no-reply@aformix.com.

• Error: "Connection timeout / port blocked"
  -> If port 465 doesn't connect on your network, change to:
     SMTP_PORT=587

• Emails going to Spam:
  -> In Hostinger hPanel, make sure your domain's SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records
     are enabled (Hostinger enables them automatically by default with 1 click
     under Email -> Manage -> DNS / Deliverability).
